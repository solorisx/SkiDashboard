About This Service

Ski Dashboard is a personal ski activity tracking service operated from Switzerland and hosted on Railway. We help you track and analyze your skiing activities by connecting with your Strava account.

Information We Collect

Account Information
  • Email address and username
  • Encrypted password
  • We never store plain text passwords
Strava Integration
  • Ski-related activities only
  • GPS tracks and timestamps
  • Ski area and lift usage data
  • No private activities
Technical Information

We collect minimal technical data: session cookies for login, security tokens, and server logs for maintenance.
