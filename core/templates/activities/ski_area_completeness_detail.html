About Lift Types
Completeness calculation

Your lift completeness percentage is calculated based on all available lift types in this ski area. This includes chairlifts, gondolas, funiculars, surface lifts, and other aerial transportation. The type of lift is determined by the aerialway tag in OpenStreetMap. If a lift does not have the correct type, I encourage you to contribute to OpenStreetMap and add or correct the aerialway tag for better data quality.

Adding filters

You can exclude specific lift types from your completeness calculations in your profile preferences. For example, if you prefer to skip surface lifts such as platter lifts, magic carpets or rope tows, you can exclude them in your preferences.
